Frequently Asked

Why is my score different on mobile vs PC?

Mobile displays and PC monitors have different hardware touch polling rates and refresh frequencies (60Hz vs 144Hz/240Hz). This can cause a 15–30ms hardware variance.

How does the challenge link work?

When you tap "Challenge a Friend", your 5-round score is encoded directly into a shareable link. No account or database is needed—anyone opening your link instantly plays against your target!
