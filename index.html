How To Play & Win

A simple test of neural processing speed. Here is how you get the best score:

1

Wait For Green

Once you start, the arena turns bold red. Breathe, maintain focus, and watch the screen. Do not anticipate or click early, or you'll trigger a penalty!

2

Tap As Fast As You Can

The instant the screen snaps to emerald green, click, tap your mobile screen, or slam the Spacebar. We measure your reaction down to the exact millisecond using high-precision timers.

3

Challenge Your Friends

Complete 5 rounds to unlock your final average rating. Use our 1-click challenge button to share your link on WhatsApp, Discord, or Instagram and dare anyone to beat it!
